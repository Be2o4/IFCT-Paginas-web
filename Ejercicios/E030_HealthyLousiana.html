Changes to Medicaid Health Plans in 2026

Louisiana Medicaid will not offer UnitedHealthcare beginning April 1, 2026. Learn more about this update.

Questions about your Louisiana Medicaid eligibility?

Please call Louisiana Medicaid customer service at 1-888-342-6207, Monday – Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. You can also take action on your Medicaid eligibility any time at MyMedicaid.la.gov.

Get the Free Mobile App

To get the app, search for Healthy Louisiana on Google Play or the App Store. Use the app to choose a health plan and provider.

You can also:

  • Use the app in Spanish
  • Find providers based on your current location
  • Get directions to providers
  • And more!

Learn more about the free mobile app on the FAQs page.

New Healthy Louisiana Members

If you want to change your health plan or dental plan so your new plan starts July 1, 2026, you must make the change before 6 p.m. on June 29, 2026. If you make the change after June 29 your new health plan or dental plan will start on August 1, 2026.
